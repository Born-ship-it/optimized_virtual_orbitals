PySCF gives me an opportunity to validate my implementation of full space MP2 by comparing with their built-in MP2 module, \code{pyscf.mp.MP2}. I present the deviation $\Delta E_{\rm corr} = E_{\rm corr}^{\rm code} - E_{\rm corr}^{\rm PySCF}$ is within numerical precision for all test molecules in cc--pVDZ, see Figure~\ref{tab:validation}. 

\begin{table}[ht]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{lccr}
        \hline
        Molecule & $E_{\rm corr}^{\rm OVOS}$ (Hartree) & $E_{\rm corr}^{\rm PySCF}$ (Hartree) & $\Delta E_{\rm corr}$ (Hartree) \\
        \hline  
        CO       & -2.90778E-01 & -2.90778E-01    & 4.25617E-8   \\
        H$_2$O   & -2.04003E-01 & -2.04003E-01    & 2.29192E-9   \\
        HF       & -2.03781E-01 & -2.03781E-01    & 1.23463E-10  \\
        NH3      & -1.88825E-01 & -1.88825E-01    & 8.31300E-10  \\
        Li2      & -2.00076E-02 & -2.00076E-02    & 1.11200E-9   \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Validation of the OVOS implementation against PySCF \code{mp.MP2}.
    Deviations $\Delta E$ are within numerical precision ($|\delta E| <\eqsim 10^{-8}$~Hartree). All molecules are of the cc--pVDZ basis for this validation.}
    \label{tab:validation}
\end{table}

It is observed that CO's deviation is barely at acceptable range of $10^{-8}$~Hartree, on the other hand, the other molecules show deviations below the acceptable range, which confirms that my implementation of the MP2 correlation energy is correct, and that the OVOS code correctly reproduces the full-space MP2 limit when $N'_{\rm act.unocc} = N_{\rm act.unocc}$.

Verifying that the full-space MP2 energies match PySCF's results, any differences observed in the OVOS results are due to the orbital optimization and active space truncation, rather than a bug in the MP2 implementation.
